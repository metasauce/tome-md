---
title: Pisco Sour
serves: 1
prep: 10 minutes
---

![Pisco Sour](assets/pisco-sour.svg)

## Ingredients

- 60 ml pisco
- 30 ml fresh lime juice
- 20 ml simple syrup
- 1 egg white
- Ice
- 3 dashes Angostura bitters
- 1 lime wheel, to garnish

## Method

1. Chill a coupe glass.
2. Dry-shake the pisco, lime juice, syrup and egg white without ice.
3. Add ice and shake again until well chilled.
4. Strain into the chilled glass and let the foam settle.
5. Add the bitters and garnish with the lime wheel.

> For a vegan version, use aquafaba instead of egg white.

**Tip:** Fresh lime juice makes the biggest difference.
